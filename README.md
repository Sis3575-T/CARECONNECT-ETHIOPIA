# CARECONNECT-ETHIOPIA
AI-Powered Smart Healthcare Referral and Patient Coordination System (Version 1.0)

CareConnect Ethiopia is a comprehensive healthcare referral and patient coordination platform designed to improve communication, referral management, and coordination between health centres, regional hospitals, transportation units, and administrative health authorities across Ethiopia.

## Key Features & 4 Portals
1. **Health Centre Portal** (e.g. Dr. Alemayehu)
   - Referral creation with AI OCR text extraction and clinical summarization
   - Completeness validation & category classification
   - Hospital ranking recommendation based on required service, distance, and real-time facility workload
   - Patient tracking and referral lifecycle management

2. **Hospital Portal** (e.g. Dr. Fitsum - Tikur Anbessa / Black Lion Hospital)
   - Real-time incoming referral queue and triage
   - Acknowledge, accept, or reject referrals with structured clinical reason
   - Additional information requests
   - Bed & service capacity management with last-updated timestamp
   - Referral outcomes and hospital feedback

3. **Transport Coordinator Portal** (e.g. Abebe Kebede)
   - Ambulance and transport dispatch coordination
   - Real-time status tracking (Requested -> Assigned -> In Transit -> Arrived)
   - Interactive route map (e.g., Yeka HC to Tikur Anbessa)
   - Delay alert reporting and arrival confirmations

4. **System Administrator Portal** (System Admin)
   - System-wide analytics and referral volume monitoring
   - Facility registration, geolocation (Region, Zone, Woreda)
   - User roles & RBAC security
   - AI model performance, OCR/NLP accuracy monitoring, and audit trails
